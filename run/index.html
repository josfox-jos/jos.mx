Open a .jos document

A link to a .jos is a proposal, not a command. The host you open it with fetches the document, checks that it hashes to the art_ in the link, shows you what it is and what it may do, and runs nothing until you approve. This page is the no-account carrier: it reads the link from the part of the address after #, which browsers never send to any server, and it has no approve button of its own.
